# Holiday Pop-Up Card: Project Constraints

- **Art Direction**: Full-viewport Three.js WebGL2 painterly gouache and stipple paper-tooth grain shaders (`createGouacheMaterial`, `PainterlyPostShader`, `createBackdropMaterial`), paired with crisp white paper cardstock and holly red, evergreen, and flat gold ink accents. Do not add harsh black vector outlines or gold UI background gradients.
- **3D Gouache Preloader & Scalloped Postage-Stamp Bouncy-Ribbon Cover**: Opens with a mandatory ~2.6s watercolor-paper (`#f7f5f0`) preloader (`LoadingOverlay.tsx`) whose square study renders the real 3D stepped 7-tier sliceform tree with 11 randomly chosen 3D ornaments through `createGouacheMaterial` and `PainterlyPostShader` (plus cotton grain coat), wiping color left-to-right before fading into the card. Reveals a paper-white (`#faf7f3`) closed card inside a scalloped crimson postage-stamp border (`#b33627`), framed with thick lush brush-painted evergreen pine sprigs, **6 actual spring-bouncy 3D velvet-crimson ribbon bows + weaving ribbon garlands** (`cityDiorama.ts` `triggerCoverBowBounce` + `playRibbonBounce` on click), `"Merry Christmas"` in **`Fleur De Leah`** centered with `"from Anna"` subtext (`index.html` must use exact capitalization `Fleur+De+Leah` plus a DOM ref so canvas renders `'Fleur De Leah'` reliably). Uses a red ribbon bow SVG favicon and static 1200x630 OpenGraph card preview (`/og-card.png`) over winter blue sky. Snowfall detours cleanly outside the closed cover perimeter (`closedHaloMix`). The opening popup uses `Glass Antiqua` across all typography (`OpeningOverlay.tsx`), unfolding the L-Fold pop-up card over ~1.45s upon submission.
- **Pure L-Fold + Thick Die-Cut Paper-Cut Landmarks**: Uses L-Fold only, with per-city winter inner walls (`Toronto`, `Seoul`, `Vancouver`), `"let it snow"` wall header and `"Season's Greetings / For [Name]"` deck plate. Every die-cut cutout builds solid extruded paper side walls (`buildPaperThicknessGeometry`) with UV-flipped back planes (`u = 1 - u` on `backGeo`) so asymmetrical silhouettes like Seoul's flying magpie keep unbroken outlines in both flight directions; Vancouver includes random animated Gastown Steam Clock puffs, and Toronto features the downtown Financial District skyline and the hunched wide-eyed ash-grey Toronto raccoon.
- **Papery-White Textured UI + Hover Effects + Bottom-Right Controls**: All UI surfaces use crisp textured cotton-white cold-press paper (`--paper-pure`) with SVG-displaced ragged `#ffffff` rims (`#gouache-deckle`) and green/red border & ink active states (`::before`). Includes hover scale/tilt transitions on ornaments in both `OrnamentTray` and the 3D tree, bottom-right 4 circular accessibility controls, and an expandable Music volume slider popover (`0-100%`, default `78%`).
- **Quirky 3D Ornaments (24 Charms, 36 360-Degree Perch Spots)**: Empty 7-tier stepped sliceform paper tree with 36 perch pads wrapping front, left/right sides, and back; unlimited drag/tap placement of all 24 ornaments (without golden top rings on tray icons) plus `Auto-Decorate` and `Clear`.
- **Collaboration**: Ask the user before making any unapproved design, architecture, or style change.
